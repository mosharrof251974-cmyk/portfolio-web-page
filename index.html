About me

Software that understands the money behind the work.

I started from the numbers. Every business I build for, whether a knitting factory, a fish farm or a household, comes down to the same questions: what does it cost, what does it earn, and where is the cash going? My software answers those questions every day.

Today I build web apps, prepare bankable project profiles for fund sourcing, and explore how AI can make planning, costing and reporting faster for small and mid-sized businesses. I write about all of it so the knowledge spreads.

Finance lab

Try it: when does a project start making profit?

Every project profile I prepare answers this question. The break-even point is the sales volume where income exactly covers all costs. Below it you lose money; above it every unit adds profit.

Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ (Price per unit − Variable cost per unit)

Change the example numbers to see how price and cost move the target. The figures are a sample fish-farm cycle in Taka, not real client data.
