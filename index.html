What every profile includes

Executive summaryThe project at a glance and the headline financial indicators a banker reads first.
Promoters & companySponsor background, track record, capital structure and shareholding.
Location, building & layoutSite advantages, building specification and floor-wise process flow.
Machinery & capacityMachine schedule, product range, installed capacity and utilisation plan.
Project cost & means of financeCapital expenditure, working capital, equity and term-loan split.
Revenue model & assumptionsPricing basis, operating costs and manpower, each assumption stated openly.

Mortgage calculator: what will your home loan really cost?

A home loan is repaid in equal monthly instalments (EMI). In the early years most of each instalment goes to interest; later, most of it repays the principal. A small change in rate or term moves the total cost by lakhs.

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

Here P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12), and n is the number of months. Change the example figures to compare options before you talk to a bank.
